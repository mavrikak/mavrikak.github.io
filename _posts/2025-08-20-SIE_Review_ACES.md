I’m delighted to share my latest publication, "Surface Integral Equations in Computational Electromagnetics: A Comprehensive Overview of Theory, Formulations, Discretization Schemes and Implementations," which was released some time ago! This work presents a thorough and rigorous exploration of the surface integral equation (SIE) method—an area foundational to high-precision electromagnetic modeling.

I’m incredibly grateful to my co-author, Prof. Olivier J.F. Martin, for his contribution and support in this work.

Whether you’re focused on antennas, microwaves, photonics, acoustics or computational methods more broadly, this review can provide both new and senior researchers with a robust reference on theory, formulations, and implementations!

Looking forward to engaging in insightful and constructive discussions. The full article is available <a href="https://journals.riverpublishers.com/index.php/ACES/article/view/27091">here</a> for anyone interested in digging deeper.
